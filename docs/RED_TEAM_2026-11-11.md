## 2. What changed

**A. The 4–5 Nov refund spike is explained (mostly).**
- **FACT:** 41,457 of the 42,330 refunds on 4–5 Nov (98%) have `refundReason = INTENT_SUBMIT_FAILED`. That reason appears only 103 times on all other days combined (`refunds_by_day_reason.csv`).
- **FACT:** 98% of the spike's refunds (41,456 of 42,330) come from three Aurora referral codes ending in `:pda`. They are `aurora-hfmj8cn56c:pda`, `aurora-widget-cmrub3ehj01g20cjz6ruuom6i:pda` and `aurora-vyppl6kbmm:pda`, and 94–97% of their swaps on those days failed. It started at 13:00 UTC on 4 Nov (`refunds_spike_by_hour.csv`, `refunds_spike_by_referral.csv`).
- **FACT:** Almost all of these were deposits from the Intents balance refunded to the Intents balance (`INTENTS → INTENTS`). They came from only 265–319 refund addresses, and the top 100 addresses account for 98–99% of the refunds. The median size was $10.
- **FACT:** Aurora's *successful* swaps stayed at their normal ~1,000/day (960 on 4 Nov, 1,068 on 5 Nov), so the failures were **extra attempts on top**, not lost demand (`aurora_referrals_by_day.csv`).
- **INFERENCE:** An Aurora-side integration retried failing intent submissions in a loop. This is not a 1Click outage and not a user exodus. It inflates "all-status swaps" on those days but does not touch volume or user counts, which use SUCCESS only. Aurora has not publicly confirmed the cause (not found).
- Side finding (**FACT**): the refund-reason labels change over time. `QUOTE_EXPIRED` first appears on 28 Oct, and `AMOUNT_LESS_THAN_MIN_AMOUNT_OUT` stops on 4 Nov. Do not compare refund reasons across that boundary.

**B. Value capture is bigger than our 1Click-only figure suggested.**

It totals **$2.56M** over 38 days (30 Sep–7 Nov) → about **$24.6M/yr** annualised. *(Superseded: the build uses a fixed 30-day window, 10 Oct–8 Nov, which gives $22.6M/yr; see `data/derived/metrics.json`.)*
- **FACT:** Our 1Click buyback-fee estimate for the same 38 days is $0.57M, which is 22% of DefiLlama's figure. That is consistent with 1Click being ~29% of volume.
- So **"$5.4M a year" is 1Click-only**. NEAR's captured Intents revenue is roughly **$25M a year**, about **5 bps** of all-Intents volume over the same days. The 1Click buyback take is about 4 bps, which is consistent.
- **FACT** (DefiLlama methodology text): since 23 Feb 2026, captured revenue "is used to buy back $NEAR on the open market (not burned)". The wallet balance in §4 tests this.

## 6. Correction, 12 Nov 2026: the scorecard verdict depended on one window

- **What an outside review found:**
  - The `buyback_outflows` signal used `wallet_largest_outflow_share_90d`. Its 90-day window started about 12 Aug, so it left out the only outflow: 12,000 NEAR on 10 Jul, 2.26% of that day's balance (`q30`).
  - Scored that way, the signal was the scorecard's only green. Over all daily data (from 1 Jul) the same measure is 0.0226, above the 0.01 bearish line, so it is red. The verdict rule (more red than green → bearish) would then have printed "bearish".
  - `thesis.yaml` also said the thresholds were set "before seeing how they would score". That was not accurate: its own note on this signal mentioned the 10 Jul move.
- **What we changed:**
  - The signal now uses `wallet_outflow_share_of_inflows`: all NEAR that left / all NEAR that came in, 1 Jul–9 Nov.
    - FACT: 12,000 / 1,754,184 = **0.68%**.
    - Without the 9 Sep treasury transfer: **0.96%**.
  - Both are yellow under the unchanged thresholds. The scorecard is now **0 green, 7 yellow, 0 red**, and the verdict "working but not yet sufficient" is unchanged.
  - The `thesis.yaml` header now says the thresholds were set after looking at the data.
- **What we did not change, and why:**
  - We kept the thresholds and the verdict rule. Loosening the bearish trigger after seeing the data would be the same mistake.
  - The old 90-day metric stays in `metrics.json` for disclosure.
  - The ex-9-Sep figure, 0.96%, sits just under the 1% bearish line. Today, about 510 more NEAR leaving would turn this signal red. That margin grows by about 1% of each day's inflow.

## A. Strongest new findings

1. **The "20 bps minimum" puzzle is mostly explained (FACT + INFERENCE, medium-high).** The fee docs (read 12 Nov) give 1Click's share a 20 bps floor, with a 1 bp tier for USDC, USDT and DAI pairs and same-asset multichain routes. The red-team log's fee-unit sample is stablecoin-to-stablecoin swaps, and its most common buyback fee is 1 bp. Still open: whether the rule applied in Sep-Nov (the pages are undated), why most swaps pay the buyback wallet nothing (only 36% do), and where 1Click's share goes on the rest.
2. **The 9 Sep treasury transfer is treated the same way by an outside source (INFERENCE, medium).** On 19 Oct, NearBlocks showed 2,015,496 NEAR in the buyback wallet and NEAR's dashboard showed 1,510,411. The gap is 505,086 NEAR = 502,793 (the transfer) + 2,293 (about 6 hours of average inflow). So the dashboard appears to count fee flow only, as the thesis does. Not confirmed by NEAR.
3. **Part of "captured revenue" is a website fee (FACT + INFERENCE, medium).** The fee docs send the 0.2% near-intents.org fee to `fefundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near`. That wallet gained about 119,540 NEAR in the 28 days to 4 Nov, about $5.8M a year at $3.73. The gap between DefiLlama revenue and buyback-wallet inflow is $6.6M a year. Most of the gap is probably this fee, which is a distribution-channel fee rather than a take on all Intents volume.
4. **58% and 18% are the same arithmetic on different bases (INFERENCE, reproduced).** 15.6 bps of $123.8M/day over 32.8M NEAR a year at $3.73 gives 58%: gross fees, a ceiling NEAR does not receive. Captured revenue (5.0 bps) gives 18%, which is the thesis's own `captured_revenue_to_issuance_usd` and the same size as Blockworks' ~18% buyback offset. The buyback wallet alone is 11% in NEAR.
5. **Capture-rate readings agree once windows are matched (FACT).** Recent 30-day shares are 28.4% (Tokenpost, 21 Oct, dates not given), about 31% (DefiLlama to 19 Oct, via Altcoin Buzz) and 32% (this thesis, to 8 Nov). The lifetime share was 16.4% on 6 Oct. A judge who compares our 32% with the lifetime figure will think the thesis is wrong unless the window is stated beside it.
6. **Custody release decides whether even the bull case shrinks the float (INFERENCE, ad-hoc run; table in section F).** At 100% release a year, the bull case's float grows instead of shrinking, although buybacks are 4.6 times issuance.
